features

Everything from Plaud — read, searched and reused on your Mac

A small, fast, native SwiftUI app. It talks to Plaud's official third-party API, read-only, and caches locally so reopening is instant and works offline.

📂

Recordings list

Every recording, grouped into Today / This week / Earlier, with live search and a local-cache indicator.

📝

AI summary & notes

The AI summary plus all note types via a dropdown — rich Markdown with task checkboxes, tables, and inline images you can save.

🗣️

Transcription

Three views: Raw (timestamps & speakers), Polished, and an interactive Outline — click a chapter to reveal its transcript.

📊

Speaker stats

Who spoke and for how long — talk time, percentage and number of interventions, with progress bars.

👥

Browse by personNEW

Flip the sidebar to People: a directory of speakers built from your meetings. Pick a person to see every recording they took part in.

🔄

Notion sync

One-way Plaud → Notion, incremental (diff by hash). Pushes every note in full and uploads images as persistent Notion files.

📄

Export to Word

Export any note to .docx with its layout and embedded images — generated natively, no dependencies.

🌍

Localized & themed

English, French and Chinese, plus follow-system language. Light, Dark or System appearance.

🔒

Private by design

Read-only on Plaud, Notion token in the Keychain, and no telemetry — nothing leaves your Mac except calls to Plaud and Notion.

why it exists

Made for people who live on a Mac

Plaud captures your meetings beautifully — but if you want to read, search, archive and reuse that content on macOS, you're stuck switching contexts. Plaud Companion is a featherweight document app that puts every recording, note and transcript one keystroke away, and now lets you pivot the whole library by who was in the room.

how it works

Your voice notes, wherever you work

Authenticate once with your own Plaud session, read everything on your Mac, and optionally mirror it into Notion — incrementally, so you never re-copy the same note twice.

get started

Up and running in a minute

No App Store, no sign-up. Notarized by Apple — it launches by double-click, even offline.

Download

Grab the latest signed .dmg from GitHub Releases and drag Plaud Companion into /Applications.

Authenticate

The app reuses your Plaud session from ~/.plaud/tokens-mcp.json (created via the Plaud MCP login). Paste it in Settings if needed.

Browse

Your recordings appear instantly. Switch to People to explore them by speaker, or open Settings → Notion to sync.

Authentication. Plaud Companion never asks for your Plaud password — it reuses the OAuth session created by the Plaud MCP server and refreshes it automatically. Speaker labels come from Plaud's diarization; rename speakers in Plaud and hit Refresh to get real names everywhere — including the People directory.
